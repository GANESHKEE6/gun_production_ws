# Weapon Manual: M134 Rotary Gatling Gun

## Overview
The Rotary Gatling Gun (Minigun) is an electrically driven, six-barrel rotary machine gun. It uses an external electric motor to rotate the barrels and cycle the actions, achieving unmatched rates of fire.

## Technical Specifications
- **Compatible Calibers**: 7.62x51mm NATO.
- **Safety Compatibility Warning**: DO NOT load hand-linked cartridges or dirty ammunition. The rotary delinking mechanism operates at 50 rounds per second; any link deformation or feed latency will cause immediate mechanical timing lock, motor stall, and potential gear shearing.
- **Base Rate of Fire**: 3000 rounds per minute (RPM).
- **Muzzle Velocity**: 860 m/s (with standard 7.62mm NATO).

## Component Compatibility & Specifications

### 1. Barrel Options
- **Short Micro-Barrels (18.0 inch)**: Reduces weapon assembly weight and speeds up electric motor spin-up time to 0.4 seconds. Widens pellet-like bullet spread. Effective range: 400 meters.
- **Standard Rotary Barrels (22.0 inch)**: Six-barrel cluster with clamp rings. Standard spin-up time of 0.8 seconds. Standard dispersal profile. Effective range: 800 meters.
- **Heavy Enclosed Barrels (26.0 inch)**: Barrels encased in an armored cooling shroud. Slow spin-up time (1.2 seconds) due to high rotational inertia. Dampens vibration and vertical rise, reducing recoil by 20%. Effective range: 1200 meters.

### 2. Stock Options
- **Dual Spade Grips**: Mounted directly behind the rotor housing with dual butterfly triggers. Offers steady hand-held support from a stationary mount.
- **Heavy Turret Mount**: Bolt-locked vehicular or tripod pedestal mount. Reduces recoil by 45% and eliminates weapon sway.
- **Tactical Carrying Harness (Predator Rig)**: A custom over-the-shoulder carrying harness with hand-held joystick trigger. Maximizes mobility but increases muzzle climb and recoil sway by 30%.

### 3. Optics Options
- **Ring Iron Sights**: Large circular aircraft-style sights. Simple alignment for tracking rapid aircraft or vehicles.
- **Holographic Sight**: Tactical red-dot sight. Helps center the high-speed bullet stream onto ground targets.
- **No Sights (Tracer Guided)**: Operators rely entirely on the visual tracer stream. Speeds up transition times.

### 4. Ammunition Profiles
- **7.62mm Linkless Chute**: Feeds ammunition from a heavy backpack or container through a flexible metal chute. Avoids link jams. Standard damage.
- **7.62mm Tracer Stream (2:1 Mix)**: Every second round is a tracer. Creates a continuous solid line of fire. Increases aiming precision by 40% under sustained fire.
- **7.62mm AP Rotary Belt**: High-velocity armor-piercing bullets. Shreds light cover and body armor, but heats the barrel cluster 30% faster.

## Step-by-Step Assembly Instructions
1. **Housing Assembly**: Place the heavy steel main rotor housing on the assembly stand.
2. **Feeder/Delinker**: Bolt the high-speed rotary delinker feed guide to the receiver intake port.
3. **Electric Motor Fit**: Align and bolt the 24V electric drive motor to the rear clutch face of the rotor housing.
4. **Barrel Cluster Insertion**: Slide the six precision barrels through the front rotor guide plate holes one by one.
5. **Rotary Clamp Locking**: Align the barrel lock grooves. Bolt the circular barrel clamp plate to the muzzle end to hold all six barrels rigid.
6. **Feeder Chute Connector**: Hook the flexible stainless-steel ammunition feed chute to the delinker assembly port.
7. **Control Grips Mount**: Install the spade handgrip assembly onto the rear receiver cap, tightening the center locking bolt.
8. **Battery Cable Connection**: Connect the heavy-duty power lines from the battery pack to the motor relay switch and trigger wire harness.

## Ballistics Performance Formulas
- **Accuracy Score**: Base (40) + Optics Bonus (Holo: +10, Rings: +2, NoSights: +5) + Barrel Length (HeavyEnclosed: +15, Short: -10) + Ammo Modifier (Tracer: +20, AP: 0)
- **Recoil Value**: Base (90) + Stock Factor (Turret: -40, Spade: -10, Harness: +25) + Spin-up Delay (Short: -5, Heavy: +10)
- **Muzzle Damage**: Base (45) + Ammo Modifier (AP: +5, Tracer: -2)
- **Effective Range**: Base (800m) * Barrel Factor (HeavyEnclosed: 1.3, Short: 0.6)
