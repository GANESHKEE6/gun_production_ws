# Weapon Manual: Bolt-Action Sniper Rifle

## Overview
The Bolt-Action Sniper Rifle is a precision weapon system designed for extreme long-range engagements. It prioritizes single-shot lethality, accuracy, and structural rigidity over rate of fire.

## Technical Specifications
- **Compatible Calibers**: .338 Lapua Magnum, 7.62x51mm NATO.
- **Safety Compatibility Warning**: NEVER attempt to chamber small 5.56x45mm rounds or shotgun shells in a .338 caliber rifle. It will cause head-space failures, cartridge rupture, and gas blowback.
- **Base Rate of Fire**: 30 rounds per minute (RPM) (Manual bolt-action cycle).
- **Muzzle Velocity**: 870 m/s (with standard .338 Lapua Magnum and Standard barrel).

## Component Compatibility & Specifications

### 1. Barrel Options
- **Short Barrel (20.0 inch)**: Compact precision barrel. Easier to carry but increases recoil by 15% and drops muzzle velocity. Effective range: 700 meters.
- **Standard Barrel (24.0 inch)**: Standard police/military sniper barrel. Excellent thermal dissipation and harmonic balance. Effective range: 1000 meters.
- **Heavy Match Barrel (27.0 inch)**: Long, fluted, heavy-contour barrel. Maximizes projectile spin stability, decreases recoil by 20%, and extends effective range to 1400 meters.

### 2. Stock Options
- **Heavy Sniper Stock**: Fully adjustable tactical chassis. Adjustable cheek riser and buttpad. Heaviest option, but absorbs 25% recoil and provides absolute stability.
- **Tactical Stock**: Lightweight skeletonized folding chassis. Reduces overall weapon weight, but offers standard recoil mitigation.
- **Classic Wood Stock**: Walnut wood stock. Solid feel, heavy weight, provides good natural dampening (-10% recoil) but lacks custom adjustability.

### 3. Optics Options
- **ACOG Scope (4x)**: Small tactical scope. Used for scout roles. Fast target acquisition, but severely limits extreme long-range viability.
- **Sniper Scope (12x-24x Variable)**: High-precision optics with parallax adjustment and illuminated Mil-Dot reticle. Boosts long-range accuracy by 80%.
- **Thermal Sniper Scope**: Displays thermal signatures of targets. Perfect for night operations. Heavy and slightly less optical clarity than standard scopes (+50% accuracy).

### 4. Ammunition Profiles
- **.338 Lapua Standard (250 gr FMJ)**: Standard match-grade round. Devastating kinetic energy, high velocity, and extreme accuracy.
- **.338 Lapua AP (Armor Piercing)**: Tungsten-carbide core. Bypasses 85% of armor plates and cover, high damage, but increases wear on match barrels.
- **7.62x51mm NATO (Light Round)**: Fits the receiver via adapter. Reduces muzzle velocity and damage by 20%, but drops vertical recoil by 40% for faster follow-up shots.

## Step-by-Step Assembly Instructions
1. **Chassis Setup**: Secure the main sniper chassis/stock in the soft-jawed assembly clamp.
2. **Receiver Placement**: Drop the heavy bolt-action receiver into the chassis inletting, lining up the action screw holes. Torque action screws to 65 in-lbs.
3. **Trigger Installation**: Pin the match trigger assembly to the bottom of the receiver. Adjust pull weight to a crisp 2.5 lbs.
4. **Barrel Fitment**: Thread the heavy match barrel into the receiver threads. Verify head-spacing using "Go" and "No-Go" precision gauges. Torque barrel nut to 75 ft-lbs.
5. **Bolt Insertion**: Insert the cylindrical bolt assembly into the rear of the receiver, checking that the extractor claw is properly aligned.
6. **Bipod Attachment**: Mount the adjustable folding bipod to the forward spigot or Picatinny rail of the chassis.
7. **Magazine Well**: Insert the single-stack box magazine into the magwell, locking the primary latch.
8. **Optic Scope Mount**: Install the heavy-duty scope ring mounts onto the receiver Picatinny rail. Set the Sniper Scope inside the rings, align vertical crosshairs, and torque ring caps to 15 in-lbs.

## Ballistics Performance Formulas
- **Accuracy Score**: Base (95) + Optics Bonus (SniperScope: +35, ACOG: +10) + Barrel Length (HeavyMatch: +15, Short: -5)
- **Recoil Value**: Base (70) + Stock Factor (HeavySniper: -20, Wood: -8, Tactical: 0) + Caliber Factor (7.62mm: -25, .338Lapua: 0)
- **Muzzle Damage**: Base (98) + Ammo Modifier (.338 Standard: 0, 7.62mm: -20, AP: +5)
- **Effective Range**: Base (1000m) * Barrel Factor (HeavyMatch: 1.4, Short: 0.7)
